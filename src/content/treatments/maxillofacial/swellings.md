## Comprehensive Care for Facial and Neck Swellings

Swellings in the jaw, face, or neck can be concerning. At ZDentistry, we provide thorough evaluation and expert treatment for a wide range of conditions causing facial and neck swellings.

### Common Causes of Facial/Neck Swellings

**Dental Origin**
- Dental abscesses
- Periapical cysts
- Dentigerous cysts
- Odontogenic tumors

**Salivary Gland Conditions**
- Salivary stones (sialolithiasis)
- Salivary gland infections (sialadenitis)
- Mucoceles and ranulas
- Salivary gland tumors

**Cysts and Tumors**
- Dermoid and epidermoid cysts
- Thyroglossal duct cysts
- Branchial cleft cysts
- Benign and malignant tumors

**Lymph Node Enlargement**
- Reactive lymphadenopathy (infection response)
- Chronic conditions
- Malignant involvement

### Our Diagnostic Approach

**1. Clinical Examination**
Thorough assessment of the swelling's characteristics:
- Size, shape, and location
- Consistency (soft, firm, hard)
- Mobility and tenderness
- Associated symptoms

**2. Imaging Studies**
- Panoramic X-rays
- CT scans for bone involvement
- MRI for soft tissue evaluation
- Ultrasound for superficial swellings

**3. Biopsy/Aspiration**
- Fine needle aspiration cytology (FNAC)
- Incisional or excisional biopsy
- Pathological analysis

### Treatment Options

**Conservative Management**
- Antibiotics for infections
- Anti-inflammatory medications
- Warm compresses and massage (for certain conditions)

**Surgical Treatment**
Dr. Zuben's expertise enables:
- Cyst enucleation with preservation of vital structures
- Marsupialization for large cysts
- Salivary stone removal
- Complete tumor excision
- Reconstructive procedures when needed

**Follow-Up Care**
- Regular monitoring for recurrence
- Pathology review
- Long-term management plans

### When to Seek Help

Don't ignore facial or neck swellings. Contact us if you notice:
- Any new lump or swelling
- Changes in existing swellings
- Associated pain or discomfort
- Difficulty eating, speaking, or breathing
- Numbness or tingling
