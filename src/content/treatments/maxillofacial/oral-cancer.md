## Oral Cancer: Early Detection Saves Lives

Oral cancer is a serious condition, but when detected early, treatment success rates are high. At ZDentistry, we're committed to thorough screening and comprehensive management of oral pathology.

### Understanding Oral Cancer

Oral cancer can affect the:
- Lips and tongue
- Floor of mouth
- Cheeks and gums
- Hard and soft palate
- Tonsils and throat

India has one of the highest rates of oral cancer in the world, largely due to tobacco and betel nut use. Regular screening is crucial for early detection.

### Our Screening Process

**Visual Examination**
Thorough inspection of all oral tissues, including areas you can't see yourself.

**Palpation**
Feeling for lumps or abnormalities in the mouth, jaw, and neck.

**Risk Assessment**
Discussion of your lifestyle factors and medical history.

**Advanced Diagnostics**
When needed, we may use:
- Brush biopsy for suspicious areas
- Toluidine blue staining
- Referral for imaging studies

### Warning Signs to Watch For

See us promptly if you notice:
- A sore that doesn't heal within 2 weeks
- White or red patches in the mouth
- A lump or thickening in the cheek
- Difficulty chewing or swallowing
- Numbness in the tongue or other areas
- Persistent sore throat or hoarseness
- Unexplained bleeding
- Dramatic weight loss

### Treatment and Management

**Surgical Management**
Dr. Zuben's maxillofacial surgery expertise enables:
- Excision of tumors with adequate margins
- Neck dissection when needed
- Reconstruction of defects
- Close coordination with oncology team

**Multidisciplinary Care**
We work with:
- Oncologists
- Radiation specialists
- Speech therapists
- Nutritionists

### Prevention

Reduce your oral cancer risk:
- Quit tobacco in all forms
- Limit alcohol consumption
- Practice sun protection (lips)
- Maintain good oral hygiene
- Get regular dental check-ups
