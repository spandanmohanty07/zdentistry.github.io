## Facial Cosmetic Surgery

Facial aesthetics and function are closely connected. At ZDentistry, Dr. Zuben combines surgical expertise with an understanding of facial harmony to provide cosmetic procedures that enhance both appearance and function.

### Our Facial Cosmetic Services

**Orthognathic (Corrective Jaw) Surgery**
Surgical repositioning of the jaws to correct:
- Protruding or receding jaw
- Open bite or deep bite
- Facial asymmetry
- Sleep apnea related to jaw position

*Benefits*
- Improved bite function
- Enhanced facial balance
- Better breathing
- Increased confidence

**Chin Surgery (Genioplasty)**
Reshaping the chin to improve facial profile:
- Chin advancement for weak chin
- Chin reduction for prominent chin
- Asymmetry correction
- Often combined with jaw surgery or rhinoplasty

**Facial Contouring**
Reshaping facial bones for improved aesthetics:
- Cheekbone augmentation or reduction
- Jawline contouring
- Facial feminization/masculinization

**Scar Revision**
Improving the appearance of facial scars from:
- Previous surgery
- Trauma
- Acne
- Burns

### Treatment Planning

**Comprehensive Assessment**
- Detailed facial analysis
- 3D imaging and planning
- Discussion of goals and expectations
- Realistic outcome preview

**Collaborative Approach**
For complex cases, we coordinate with:
- Orthodontists (for jaw surgery patients)
- Plastic surgeons
- ENT specialists

### What to Expect

**Consultation**
Thorough evaluation of your concerns and goals. We'll explain all options and help you make an informed decision.

**Pre-Operative Preparation**
- Medical clearance
- Dental preparation (for orthognathic cases)
- Detailed surgical planning

**Surgery**
- Performed with appropriate anesthesia
- Careful attention to aesthetics
- Minimally invasive when possible

**Recovery**
- Detailed post-operative care instructions
- Regular follow-up visits
- Support throughout healing

### Is Facial Cosmetic Surgery Right for You?

Good candidates are:
- In good general health
- Non-smokers (or willing to quit)
- Have realistic expectations
- Motivated for personal reasons
