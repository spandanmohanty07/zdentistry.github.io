## Advanced Implant Solutions for Complex Cases

Not everyone is a straightforward candidate for dental implants. At ZDentistry, Dr. Zuben's expertise in oral and maxillofacial surgery enables us to offer advanced implant procedures for patients with challenging cases.

### When Advanced Techniques Are Needed

- Significant bone loss in the jaw
- Previous failed implants
- Need for full arch restoration
- Proximity to vital structures (nerves, sinuses)
- Compromised healing (diabetes, osteoporosis)

### Our Advanced Services

**Bone Grafting**
Rebuilding lost bone to create a solid foundation for implants:

*Types of Bone Grafts*
- Autogenous grafts (your own bone)
- Allograft (donor bone)
- Xenograft (animal-derived bone)
- Synthetic bone substitutes

*Procedures*
- Socket preservation after extraction
- Ridge augmentation
- Block grafts for severe defects

**Sinus Lift Surgery**
Creating bone in the upper jaw posterior region:

- Direct sinus lift for significant augmentation
- Indirect (crestal) approach for minor lifts
- Simultaneous implant placement when possible

**Full Arch Solutions**

*All-on-4/All-on-6*
- Full arch of teeth on 4-6 implants
- Immediate loading protocols available
- Angled implants maximize available bone
- Cost-effective compared to individual implants

*Zygomatic Implants*
- Anchored in the cheekbone
- For severe maxillary bone loss
- Avoids extensive grafting

**Immediate Implant Placement**
- Implant placed same day as extraction
- Reduces treatment time
- Preserves bone and soft tissue
- Not suitable for all cases

### Technology and Techniques

**Digital Planning**
- 3D CT scan analysis
- Computer-guided surgery
- Custom surgical guides
- Predictable outcomes

**Advanced Surgical Approaches**
- Minimally invasive techniques
- Piezoelectric surgery for precise bone work
- Platelet-rich fibrin (PRF) for enhanced healing
