## Expert Care for Facial Trauma

Road traffic accidents, falls, sports injuries, and assaults can result in serious facial injuries. At ZDentistry, Dr. Zuben brings specialized training in oral and maxillofacial surgery to provide comprehensive trauma care.

### Types of Injuries We Treat

**Jaw Fractures**
- Mandible (lower jaw) fractures
- Maxilla (upper jaw) fractures
- Complex combined fractures

**Facial Bone Fractures**
- Zygomatic (cheekbone) fractures
- Orbital (eye socket) fractures
- Nasal bone fractures
- Le Fort fractures

**Soft Tissue Injuries**
- Facial lacerations
- Lip and tongue injuries
- Nerve damage
- Salivary gland injuries

**Dental Trauma**
- Avulsed (knocked-out) teeth
- Fractured teeth
- Luxated (displaced) teeth
- Alveolar bone fractures

### Our Approach to Trauma Care

**1. Emergency Stabilization**
We prioritize airway, breathing, and bleeding control before addressing specific injuries.

**2. Comprehensive Assessment**
Advanced imaging (CT scans, X-rays) helps us fully understand the extent of injuries.

**3. Surgical Repair**
Using modern techniques and materials, we restore form and function:
- Titanium plates and screws for bone fixation
- Minimally invasive approaches when possible
- Careful attention to aesthetics

**4. Reconstruction**
For severe injuries, we may perform staged reconstruction to achieve optimal results.

**5. Rehabilitation**
Post-operative care includes physiotherapy, dietary guidance, and follow-up care.
