## Restore Your Smile with Dental Implants

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. At ZDentistry, Dr. Zuben's expertise in oral and maxillofacial surgery ensures you receive the highest quality implant care.

### Why Choose Dental Implants?

**Natural Look and Feel**
Implants are designed to look, feel, and function like your natural teeth. Most people can't tell the difference.

**Preserve Bone and Facial Structure**
Unlike dentures, implants stimulate the jawbone, preventing the bone loss that occurs when teeth are missing.

**Long-Lasting Solution**
With proper care, implants can last a lifetime, making them a cost-effective long-term solution.

**Protect Adjacent Teeth**
Unlike bridges, implants don't require grinding down neighboring healthy teeth.

### The Implant Process

**1. Consultation and Planning**
We evaluate your oral health, take detailed scans, and create a personalized treatment plan.

**2. Implant Placement**
A titanium post is surgically placed into the jawbone. This serves as the root of your new tooth.

**3. Healing Period**
Over 3-6 months, the implant fuses with your bone in a process called osseointegration.

**4. Abutment Placement**
Once healed, a connector piece (abutment) is attached to the implant.

**5. Crown Placement**
Finally, a custom-made crown is attached, completing your new tooth.

### Types of Implant Solutions

- **Single Tooth Implants**: Replace one missing tooth
- **Implant-Supported Bridges**: Replace multiple adjacent teeth
- **Implant-Supported Dentures**: Secure full arch of teeth
- **All-on-4**: Full arch restoration with just four implants
