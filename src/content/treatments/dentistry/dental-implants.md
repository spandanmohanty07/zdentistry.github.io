### What Is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone where a tooth root once was. Over 3–6 months, the titanium fuses with your bone in a process called **osseointegration**, creating a stable, permanent anchor. A custom-made crown is then attached on top, looking and functioning exactly like a natural tooth.

The result: a permanent replacement tooth that you brush, floss, and forget about — just like the real thing.

Saving your natural jawbone is one of the most underappreciated benefits of implants. When a tooth is lost and not replaced, the underlying bone begins to resorb (shrink) within months. This can eventually change your facial profile, making you look older than your years. Implants are the only tooth replacement that stimulates and preserves bone like natural teeth.

### The Dental Implant Process at ZDentistry, Cuttack

**Step 1 — Consultation and Assessment**
Dr. Zuben examines your mouth, takes digital X-rays, and where needed, a 3D cone beam CT scan to assess your bone volume and density. We discuss your health history, treatment options, and provide a clear, transparent cost estimate. There's no obligation at this stage.

**Step 2 — Treatment Planning**
A personalised implant plan is designed, including whether you need any preparatory procedures like bone grafting or a sinus lift (see our [advanced implant surgery](/treatments/maxillofacial/advanced-implants/) page for complex cases).

**Step 3 — Implant Placement**
Under local anaesthesia, the titanium implant post is placed into the jawbone through a small incision in the gum. The area is stitched, and a healing cap is placed. Most patients describe this as less uncomfortable than a tooth extraction.

**Step 4 — Osseointegration (Healing Phase)**
Over the next 3–6 months, the implant fuses completely with your bone. During this time, a temporary restoration may be provided if the gap is visible.

**Step 5 — Crown Placement**
Once the implant is fully integrated, we attach a custom-made permanent crown matched precisely to the colour and shape of your surrounding teeth.

### Types of Implant Solutions at ZDentistry

- **Single tooth implant** — for one missing tooth
- **Implant-supported bridge** — for two or more adjacent missing teeth (supported by two implants, no need to grind healthy teeth)
- **Implant-retained denture** — full denture secured by 2–4 implants, preventing slipping and bone loss
- **All-on-4/All-on-6** — full arch of fixed teeth on just 4 or 6 implants, often with immediate loading

For patients with significant bone loss or complex anatomy, Dr. Zuben's maxillofacial surgical training allows him to offer bone grafting, sinus lifts, and other advanced techniques in-house — procedures that many general dental clinics in Cuttack and Odisha cannot provide.

### Who Is a Good Candidate for Dental Implants?

Most healthy adults are suitable candidates. Ideal candidates:
- Are in good general health
- Have adequate jawbone volume (or are willing to undergo grafting)
- Have healthy gums
- Are non-smokers (or prepared to stop before and after surgery)
- Are committed to excellent oral hygiene and regular check-ups
