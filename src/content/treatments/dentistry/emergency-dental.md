## Emergency Dental Care When You Need It Most

Dental emergencies don't follow a schedule. At ZDentistry, we understand the urgency of dental pain and problems, and we're committed to providing prompt care when you need it most.

### Common Dental Emergencies

**Severe Toothache**
Persistent, severe tooth pain often indicates infection or advanced decay. Don't try to wait it out - early treatment prevents complications.

**Knocked-Out Tooth**
Time is critical! A knocked-out permanent tooth can often be saved if you act quickly:
1. Find the tooth
2. Hold it by the crown only
3. Rinse gently - don't scrub
4. Try to reinsert it or keep it in milk
5. Call us immediately

**Broken or Chipped Tooth**
Save any pieces, rinse your mouth, and apply cold compress to reduce swelling. See us as soon as possible to prevent further damage.

**Lost Filling or Crown**
Cover the exposed area with dental cement or sugar-free gum and avoid chewing on that side. Schedule an appointment promptly.

**Abscess**
A painful, pus-filled infection that can spread. Signs include severe pain, swelling, fever, and bad taste. This requires immediate treatment.

### Preventing Dental Emergencies

While not all emergencies can be prevented, you can reduce your risk:
- Maintain regular dental check-ups
- Wear a mouthguard during sports
- Avoid chewing hard objects (ice, popcorn kernels, etc.)
- Don't use teeth as tools
- Address dental problems before they become emergencies
