## Keep Your Smile Healthy with Regular Check-ups in Cuttack

At ZDentistry in CDA Sector-9, Cuttack, we believe prevention is always better — and far less expensive — than cure. Regular dental check-ups are the single most effective thing you can do to protect your long-term oral health. Many serious dental problems develop silently, with no pain, until they reach an advanced stage. A routine visit every six months lets us catch and treat issues early, before they become costly and complicated.

### What Our Comprehensive Dental Check-up Includes

When you visit ZDentistry, your check-up goes far beyond a quick look at your teeth. Dr. Zuben Mohanty (MDS) performs a thorough examination covering:

**Visual Examination**
We inspect every surface of all your teeth, your gums, tongue, inner cheeks, palate, and throat. We look for early signs of decay, gum recession, worn enamel, and oral pathology.

**Digital X-rays**
When clinically necessary, we take precise digital X-rays to detect decay between teeth, bone loss around the roots, cysts, or impacted wisdom teeth — problems that are invisible to the naked eye. Digital X-rays use significantly less radiation than traditional film X-rays.

**Gum Health Assessment (Periodontal Check)**
We measure the depth of the spaces between your teeth and gums to detect early signs of periodontal (gum) disease. Gum disease is one of the leading causes of tooth loss in adults and has well-established links to heart disease and diabetes.

**Oral Cancer Screening**
India has one of the highest rates of oral cancer in the world, and Odisha is no exception. Tobacco products, pan masala, and betel nut — widely used in Cuttack and surrounding areas — are major risk factors. At ZDentistry, every check-up includes a thorough oral cancer screening. Early detection dramatically improves outcomes. Learn more about our [oral cancer screening services](/treatments/maxillofacial/oral-cancer/).

**Professional Teeth Cleaning (Scale and Polish)**
Our trained dental team removes hardened tartar (calculus) deposits from all surfaces of your teeth — buildup that no toothbrush or flossing can remove. We then polish your teeth to remove surface stains and leave your mouth feeling fresh and clean.

**Personalised Oral Hygiene Advice**
Every patient's mouth is different. We advise you on brushing technique, the right toothpaste and tools for your situation, dietary habits that affect your teeth, and how to address any specific concerns.

### Why Regular Check-ups Matter in Cuttack

Oral health habits in Cuttack — as in much of India — are impacted by high sugar consumption, tobacco use, irregular dental visits, and limited awareness of early symptoms. As a result, we frequently see patients who present with advanced decay or gum disease that could easily have been prevented with regular check-ups.

Some of the most common problems we catch during routine visits:

- **Early-stage cavities** — treated with a simple filling rather than a root canal
- **Early gum disease (gingivitis)** — reversed with professional cleaning and home care
- **Cracked teeth** — identified before they split completely
- **Early oral cancer lesions** — detected before they spread
- **Impacted wisdom teeth** — addressed before they damage neighbouring teeth
