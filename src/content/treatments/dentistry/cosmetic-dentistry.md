### Our Cosmetic Dentistry Services in Cuttack

**Professional Teeth Whitening**

Professional whitening at ZDentistry delivers results that no over-the-counter product can match. We use clinically proven whitening agents that lighten teeth by several shades safely and effectively.

- **In-clinic whitening**: Performed in a single 60-minute appointment. Results are immediate and dramatic — teeth can lighten by 6–10 shades in one session.
- **Take-home whitening**: Custom-fitted trays and professional-grade whitening gel for gradual whitening in the comfort of your home over 2 weeks.

Professional whitening is safe for your enamel when done under dental supervision. We check your teeth and gums beforehand to ensure you're a suitable candidate.

**Dental Veneers**

Veneers are ultra-thin shells of porcelain or composite resin custom-made to fit over the front surface of your teeth. They are one of the most versatile cosmetic treatments available, capable of transforming:

- Severely stained or discoloured teeth that don't respond to whitening
- Chipped, cracked, or worn teeth
- Gaps or spaces between teeth (diastema closure)
- Mildly misaligned teeth
- Teeth that are uneven in size or shape

*Porcelain veneers* are the premium option — highly durable, stain-resistant, and the most natural-looking. They require a thin layer of enamel to be removed and are fabricated in a lab over two appointments.

*Composite veneers* can be placed in a single visit and are more affordable, though they require more maintenance over time.

**Dental Bonding**

Dental bonding uses tooth-coloured composite resin applied directly to the tooth to repair chips, close small gaps, improve shape, or cover discolouration. It's one of the quickest and most affordable cosmetic improvements available, often completed in a single visit without anaesthesia.

**Tooth Reshaping and Contouring**

Minor irregularities — slightly uneven teeth, small chips, overlapping edges — can often be corrected by carefully reshaping the enamel. This is a quick, painless procedure with immediately visible results.

**Smile Makeovers**

A smile makeover is a comprehensive, personalised treatment plan that combines multiple cosmetic and restorative procedures to achieve your ideal smile. At ZDentistry in Cuttack, we design smile makeovers around your specific goals — whether that's a subtle improvement or a dramatic transformation.

A typical smile makeover might combine:
- Teeth whitening as a foundation
- Veneers on the front teeth
- Bonding to repair chips
- Gum contouring if the gumline is uneven
- Replacement of missing teeth with implants
