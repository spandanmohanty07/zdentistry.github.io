### Why Start Dental Visits Early?

Baby teeth might be temporary, but they play a critical role in your child's development. They help children eat and speak properly, guide permanent teeth into the correct position, and contribute to facial bone development. Losing them too early due to untreated decay can lead to crowding, misalignment, and speech problems.

Early childhood caries (tooth decay in babies and young children) is **very common in Cuttack** and across Odisha. Sugary drinks, biscuits, and the habit of bottle feeding with formula or juice at night are leading causes. Starting dental visits early helps us identify these risks, counsel parents, and take preventive action before decay takes hold.

### Children's Dental Services at ZDentistry, Cuttack

**Regular Check-ups and Cleaning**
We recommend a check-up every 6 months from age 1 onwards. Each visit includes a thorough examination of teeth, gums, jaw development, and bite, plus a gentle clean and polish. We monitor the eruption of baby and permanent teeth to ensure everything is on track.

**Fluoride Varnish**
Applied to teeth in seconds, fluoride varnish hardens enamel and dramatically reduces cavity risk. We recommend it every 6 months for children at moderate-to-high risk of decay. It's especially important in Cuttack where water fluoridation is not consistently implemented.

**Fissure Sealants**
The grooves on the biting surfaces of back molars are natural traps for food and bacteria. Sealants are a liquid resin that flows into these grooves and sets hard, sealing them against decay. We recommend sealants when:
- The first permanent molars erupt (~age 6–7)
- The second permanent molars erupt (~age 11–13)

The procedure is completely painless and takes just a few minutes per tooth.

**Children's Fillings**
When decay is present, we treat it promptly with tooth-coloured composite fillings. We use local anaesthesia to ensure a pain-free experience and take extra time to help anxious children feel comfortable. Baby teeth with decay must be treated — leaving them untreated causes pain, infection, and can damage the permanent tooth developing underneath.

**Space Maintainers**
If a baby tooth is lost prematurely due to trauma or extraction, a space maintainer holds the gap open to prevent adjacent teeth from drifting and crowding out the incoming permanent tooth.

**Early Orthodontic Assessment**
We monitor jaw and bite development from an early age. Identifying orthodontic issues early — such as crossbites, crowding, or thumb-sucking effects — allows for simpler, more effective treatment. We refer to orthodontists when specialist care is needed.

### Dental Health Tips for Parents in Cuttack

**By Age:**

| Age | Brushing | Toothpaste | Key Focus |
|-----|----------|-----------|-----------|
| 0–1 | Damp cloth or soft baby brush | None | Gum hygiene, first tooth care |
| 1–3 | Soft baby brush, twice daily | Rice-grain of fluoride paste | Habit forming, check-up at age 1 |
| 3–6 | Child brush, supervised | Pea-size of fluoride paste | Reduce sugar, check sealant need |
| 6+ | Adult brush, supervised until 8 | Regular fluoride paste | Monitor permanent teeth, sealants |

**Diet Advice**
- Limit biscuits, sweets, and sugary drinks to mealtimes only
- Avoid giving juice or formula in a bottle at night — it's a leading cause of early childhood caries
- Water and plain milk are the best between-meal drinks
- Raw vegetables and fruits are great for teeth

**Making it Fun**
- Let your child pick their own toothbrush in their favourite colour
- Play a 2-minute song during brushing time
- Use a reward chart for consistent brushing
- Visit the dentist regularly so it becomes normal, not frightening
