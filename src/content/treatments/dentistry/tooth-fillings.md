## Tooth Fillings in Cuttack: Fast, Painless, Natural-Looking

Tooth decay (dental caries) is one of the most common health problems worldwide, and Cuttack is no exception. High sugar consumption, inconsistent brushing habits, and infrequent dental visits mean that cavities are extremely common among patients of all ages who visit ZDentistry. The good news: caught early, cavities are simple and affordable to treat with a filling.

At ZDentistry in CDA Sector-9, Cuttack, we use modern **tooth-coloured composite resin** fillings that look completely natural, are mercury-free, and bond directly to the tooth for added strength. Say goodbye to the grey metallic fillings of the past.

### Why Cavities Need Prompt Treatment

Tooth decay is progressive. What starts as a small cavity in the outer enamel will — if left untreated — advance through the dentine and eventually reach the soft pulp at the centre of the tooth. At that point, you'll need a root canal treatment rather than a simple filling. And if the damage continues, the tooth may need to be extracted entirely.

This is why acting early matters. A filling today costs a fraction of the root canal or implant that could become necessary if you wait.

### Types of Fillings at ZDentistry, Cuttack

**Composite Resin (Tooth-Coloured) Fillings**
Our most commonly placed filling. Composite is a durable, natural-looking material that comes in a range of shades to match your tooth colour precisely.

- Completely metal and mercury-free
- Bonds chemically to the tooth structure, strengthening it
- Suitable for front and back teeth
- Completed in a single visit
- Can also repair chipped or worn teeth

**Ceramic Inlays and Onlays**
For larger cavities that a standard filling may not adequately restore, we may recommend a ceramic inlay (fits inside the cusp tips) or onlay (extends over one or more cusps). These are fabricated in a dental lab for a precise, long-lasting fit, and are virtually indistinguishable from a natural tooth.

**Glass Ionomer Cement**
Occasionally used for small cavities near the gum line or for children's teeth. Releases fluoride to help protect surrounding tooth structure.

### Our Filling Process at ZDentistry

**Step 1 — Examination and Diagnosis**
We examine the tooth carefully and may take a digital X-ray to fully understand the extent of decay.

**Step 2 — Local Anaesthesia**
We numb the area around the tooth completely. There is no drilling until you feel nothing.

**Step 3 — Removing Decay**
We carefully remove all the decayed tooth material using a dental drill and hand instruments.

**Step 4 — Placing the Filling**
The composite resin is applied in layers, with each layer hardened using a special curing light. We shape it to match your natural tooth anatomy precisely.

**Step 5 — Bite Check and Polish**
We check that your bite is even and polish the filling for a smooth, natural finish.

### Signs You Might Need a Filling

- Sensitivity to sweet, hot, or cold foods or drinks
- A visible hole or dark spot on a tooth
- Pain or discomfort when biting down
- Food repeatedly catching in one area
- A rough or sharp edge you can feel with your tongue
- A previously placed filling that has cracked or fallen out

Many cavities cause **no symptoms at all** until they become large — another reason regular [dental check-ups](/treatments/dentistry/dental-checkup/) at ZDentistry in Cuttack are so important.

### Caring for Your Fillings

Composite fillings are easy to maintain. Simply continue your normal oral hygiene routine — brush twice daily with fluoride toothpaste, floss daily, and visit ZDentistry every 6 months. Avoid biting on very hard objects (ice, pens, bottle caps). With good care, composite fillings can last 7–10 years or longer before needing replacement.
