## Restore Your Teeth with Quality Fillings

Cavities don't have to mean visible metal in your smile. At ZDentistry, we use advanced tooth-colored composite materials that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth while providing excellent durability.

### Types of Fillings We Offer

**Composite (Tooth-Colored) Fillings**
- Match your natural tooth color
- Bond directly to tooth structure
- Require less tooth removal
- Mercury-free and biocompatible

**Our Filling Process**

1. **Diagnosis**: We examine your tooth and may take X-rays to assess the extent of decay
2. **Numbing**: Local anesthesia ensures a pain-free experience
3. **Preparation**: We remove the decayed portion of the tooth
4. **Filling**: The cavity is filled with composite material and shaped to match your tooth
5. **Curing**: A special light hardens the material
6. **Polishing**: We smooth and polish for a natural finish

### Signs You May Need a Filling

- Tooth sensitivity to hot, cold, or sweet foods
- Visible holes or dark spots on teeth
- Pain when biting down
- Food getting stuck between teeth
- Rough or sharp edges on a tooth

### Don't Wait on Cavities

Untreated cavities only get worse over time and can lead to more serious (and expensive) problems like root canals or extractions. If you suspect you have a cavity, schedule an appointment today for a prompt evaluation.
