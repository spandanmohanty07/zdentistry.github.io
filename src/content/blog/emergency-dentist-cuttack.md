## What Counts as a Dental Emergency?

Many people are unsure whether their situation needs urgent attention. Here are the most common dental emergencies we treat at ZDentistry:

### 1. Severe Toothache
A toothache that does not go away, worsens with pressure, or keeps you awake at night is a dental emergency. This often signals deep decay, infection, or an abscess. **Do not ignore it** — tooth infections can spread to the jaw and neck if untreated.

### 2. Knocked-Out Tooth (Avulsed Tooth)
A tooth knocked out from trauma needs treatment within **30 to 60 minutes** for the best chance of re-implantation. Time is critical.

**What to do immediately:**
- Handle the tooth by the crown (not the root)
- Rinse gently with clean water — do not scrub
- Try to reinsert it into the socket if possible, or keep it in milk
- Call ZDentistry and come in immediately

### 3. Cracked or Broken Tooth
A cracked tooth may not always cause pain, but it can expose inner pulp to bacteria, leading to infection. A tooth broken from injury needs urgent attention to prevent further damage.

### 4. Dental Abscess
An abscess is a pocket of infection (pus) in the tooth, gums, or jaw bone. Signs include:
- Severe, throbbing pain
- Swelling of the cheek or jaw
- Fever
- Bad taste in the mouth
- Difficulty swallowing or breathing (seek hospital emergency if this occurs)

A dental abscess **will not resolve on its own** and requires professional drainage and antibiotics. Left untreated, the infection can spread to the bloodstream (sepsis) — a life-threatening condition.

### 5. Lost or Broken Filling / Crown
A lost filling or crown exposes sensitive tooth structure to hot, cold, and bacteria. This can progress to infection quickly. Come in for same-day repair.

### 6. Bleeding Gums After Injury
Significant gum bleeding from a fall or impact should be evaluated immediately. Apply gentle pressure with clean gauze while you travel to our clinic.

### 7. Soft Tissue Injuries (Lip, Cheek, Tongue)
Cuts inside the mouth from accidents can be serious. Apply pressure and come to us for assessment.

## What to Do Before You Reach ZDentistry

While on your way to our Cuttack clinic:

| Emergency | Immediate Action |
|---|---|
| Severe toothache | Rinse with warm salt water; take paracetamol (not aspirin on gum) |
| Knocked-out tooth | Store in milk or saliva; do NOT let it dry |
| Broken tooth | Rinse mouth; save any fragments |
| Abscess swelling | Cold compress on cheek; avoid warm compresses |
| Bleeding gums | Firm pressure with clean gauze |

**Avoid placing aspirin directly on gum tissue** — it causes chemical burns.

## How to Prevent Future Dental Emergencies

Many dental emergencies are preventable with regular care:

1. **See your dentist every 6 months** — small problems are caught before they become emergencies
2. **Wear a mouthguard** during contact sports
3. **Avoid using teeth as tools** — don't open bottles or bite hard packaging
4. **Treat tooth decay early** — a small cavity treated today won't become a root canal tomorrow
5. **Don't ignore tooth sensitivity** — it's often the first sign of a problem
