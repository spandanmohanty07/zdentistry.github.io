## What Is Root Canal Treatment?

Root canal treatment (also called endodontic therapy) is a dental procedure that saves a severely infected or decayed tooth. When bacteria reach the soft inner tissue (pulp) of a tooth — through a deep cavity, a crack, or injury — infection sets in. Without treatment, the tooth may need to be extracted.

## Signs You May Need a Root Canal

You might need root canal treatment if you experience:

- **Severe toothache**, especially when biting or chewing
- **Prolonged sensitivity** to hot or cold temperatures
- **Swollen or tender gums** near the affected tooth
- **Darkening** or discolouration of the tooth
- **A persistent pimple or abscess** on the gums
- **Sudden relief from pain** after it was severe (this can mean the nerve has died — still requires treatment)

## Is Root Canal Treatment Painful?

This is the most common question patients in Cuttack ask. The honest answer: **No, modern root canal treatment is not painful.**

The procedure is performed under **local anaesthesia**, which completely numbs the tooth and surrounding area. You will feel pressure but no pain. In fact, the root canal *relieves* the pain caused by infection.

## Step-by-Step: Root Canal Procedure at ZDentistry, Cuttack

### Step 1: Diagnosis & X-Ray
We take a digital X-ray to assess the extent of infection and the shape of the root canals.

### Step 2: Local Anaesthesia
The area around the tooth is completely numbed. You will be comfortable throughout the procedure.

### Step 3: Pulp Removal
A small opening is made in the crown of the tooth. The infected pulp tissue is carefully removed using fine instruments.

### Step 4: Canal Cleaning & Shaping
The root canals are cleaned, disinfected, and shaped to receive the filling material.

### Step 5: Canal Filling
The canals are filled with a biocompatible material called **gutta-percha**, then sealed.

### Step 6: Restoration (Crown Placement)
A tooth that has had a root canal is more fragile. We typically recommend placing a **dental crown** over it to restore full strength and protect it from fractures.

## Root Canal vs. Tooth Extraction: Which Is Better?

Many patients ask whether it's better to simply extract the painful tooth. Our recommendation is always to **save the natural tooth** where possible. Here's why:

| | Root Canal | Extraction |
|--|--|--|
| Preserves natural tooth | ✅ Yes | ❌ No |
| Maintains bite & alignment | ✅ Yes | Needs replacement |
| Long-term cost | Lower | Higher (implant/bridge needed) |
| Recovery time | Shorter | Longer |
| Appearance | Natural | Gap unless replaced |

A saved natural tooth is always the gold standard. Dental implants or bridges are good options when extraction is unavoidable, but they cannot fully replicate your natural tooth.

## Root Canal Aftercare Tips

After your root canal at ZDentistry in Cuttack:

1. **Avoid eating on that side** until the permanent crown is placed
2. **Take prescribed medication** as directed (antibiotics or pain relievers if given)
3. **Maintain good oral hygiene** — brush twice daily, floss gently
4. **Attend the follow-up** appointment for crown placement
5. **Call us** if you experience severe pain, swelling, or the temporary filling comes out
