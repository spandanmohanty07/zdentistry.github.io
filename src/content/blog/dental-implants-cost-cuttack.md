## What Determines the Cost of Dental Implants in Cuttack?

Dental implant costs are not one-size-fits-all. Several factors influence the total cost:

### 1. Number of Implants Needed
- **Single tooth implant**: 1 missing tooth
- **Multiple implants**: Several missing teeth (with individual crowns or a bridge)
- **Full arch (All-on-4 / All-on-6)**: Full upper or lower jaw restoration

### 2. Type of Implant System
Premium implant brands (Swiss, Korean, US-made) have higher upfront costs but offer proven long-term reliability. At ZDentistry, we use quality implant systems to ensure safe, lasting results.

### 3. Type of Crown/Restoration
- Zirconia crowns (most natural-looking, highly durable)
- PFM (porcelain-fused-to-metal)
- All-ceramic crowns

### 4. Need for Additional Procedures
Some patients require **bone grafting** (if jawbone has resorbed) or a **sinus lift** (for upper back teeth) before implant placement. These add to the total cost but are necessary for long-term implant success.

### 5. Complexity of the Case
Simple single-tooth implants are less costly. Full-mouth reconstruction with bone grafting requires more surgical expertise and planning — but at ZDentistry, we are equipped to handle all levels of complexity.

## The Dental Implant Process at ZDentistry, Cuttack

### Phase 1: Consultation & Assessment
We evaluate your oral health, take digital X-rays (and 3D cone beam CT if needed), and design your personalised treatment plan. You receive a clear, transparent cost breakdown before any work begins.

### Phase 2: Implant Placement
The titanium implant post is surgically placed into the jawbone under local anaesthesia. Most patients describe this as more comfortable than a tooth extraction.

### Phase 3: Healing (Osseointegration)
Over 3–6 months, the implant fuses with your jawbone. A temporary restoration may be provided during this period.

### Phase 4: Abutment & Crown Placement
Once healed, the connecting abutment is placed and a custom-made crown is fitted. The result is a permanent, natural-looking tooth.
